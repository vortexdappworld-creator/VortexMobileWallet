# Security Policy

Vortex is a non-custodial, open-source crypto wallet. The security of user
funds and data is our top priority. We appreciate the work of security
researchers and welcome responsible disclosure of vulnerabilities.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please report suspected vulnerabilities **privately**. Do **NOT** open public
GitHub issues, pull requests, or discussions for security reports.

- **Email:** [vortexdappworld@gmail.com](mailto:vortexdappworld@gmail.com)

To help us triage quickly, please include:

- A clear description of the issue and its potential impact.
- Step-by-step instructions to reproduce it.
- The affected platform(s) and app version (iOS / Android / Desktop / Web / Browser Extension).
- Any proof-of-concept code, logs, or screenshots — without exposing real user data, keys, or seed phrases.

We aim to acknowledge new reports within a few business days and will keep you
updated as we investigate and remediate.

## Scope

In scope:

- The mobile app (iOS / Android)
- The desktop app (macOS / Windows / Linux)
- The web app and browser extension
- Source code in this repository

Out of scope (non-exhaustive):

- Vulnerabilities in third-party dependencies that are already publicly known.
- Social engineering, phishing, or attacks requiring physical access to an unlocked device.
- Denial-of-service, spam, or volumetric attacks.
- Reports from automated scanners without a demonstrated, reproducible impact.
- Issues that require a rooted/jailbroken device or a maliciously modified build.

## Responsible Disclosure

- Give us a reasonable amount of time to investigate and fix an issue before any public disclosure.
- Make a good-faith effort to avoid privacy violations, data loss, and service disruption.
- Only interact with accounts and wallets you own or have explicit permission to test.
- Never access, modify, or exfiltrate other users' data, funds, keys, or seed phrases.

## Safe Harbor

We consider security research conducted in good faith and in accordance with
this policy to be authorized. We will not pursue legal action against
researchers who follow these guidelines. If in doubt about whether a specific
action is acceptable, contact us first at the email above.

## Protecting Your Wallet

Vortex never asks for your recovery phrase or private keys. No member of the
team will ever request them. Anyone asking for your seed phrase is attempting
to steal your funds — never share it with anyone.
